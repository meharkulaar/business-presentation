# "John's Story": a Christmas storybook (draft outline v1)

**Runtime:** about 38 minutes · **Slides:** about 32 · **Style:** storybook, red / white / silver / gold, Christmas
**Framing:** every chapter opens on a Christmas in John's life (age 40 → 90). Snow falls, the tree in the window grows taller, and the family grows.

**Recurring on-screen tracker (bottom of every story slide):**
- John's age on a timeline
- Net worth ($)
- The CDA bucket filling
- The 7 Needs: seven ornaments that light up one by one
- The 3 Rules: Compound / Risk / Tax flip from red to gold

**Characters:**
- **John** (40, dentist)
- **Emma**, his wife
- **Kids:** Lily (6) and Max (4)
- **Mehar**, the advisor
- **John's accountant** and **John's lawyer**: John brings them in and introduces them to Mehar
- **Jake**, the twin: identical family, income, age and net worth

---

## Prologue: the hook (3 min)
1. **Cover:** "The Gift That Keeps Giving: John's Story." Snow, a storybook opening.
2. **Hook:** "Most business owners will give the government the biggest gift of their life, and never mean to." The real Ontario family story: $715K RRSP → about $56K left.
3. **"Once upon a time..."** Meet John.

## Chapter 1: Christmas, age 40. The rat race (4 min)
4. John at 40: a dental clinic, revenue of $500K–$750K, starting net worth of about $1.4M (home $1.6M, clinic $900K, $250K corporate savings, $150K RRSP), a $1M practice loan ($500K still owing), a $1M home mortgage. Exhausted.
5. His goals: debt-free by 50, more locations, retire by 65.
6. The problem: cash is piling up in his corporation and taxed every year. The 3 Rules are all **red**.

## Chapter 2: The meeting (5 min)
7. John meets Mehar. The corporation "check-up": the **7 Needs**, mostly unmet.
8. The **3 hidden problems** show up in John's life: estate tax, access to capital, multiplying his wealth.
9. **The team:** John introduces his accountant and his lawyer. Everyone has a role.

## Chapter 3: The plan (6 min)
10. Why 10%: the seed and tree.
11. The plan: a permanent (whole life) policy, from the real illustration (male 40, $99,999 a year for 15 years), funded from his corporate savings plus profit (not shown as "10%" for John).
12. The loonie: a corporate dollar vs a personal dollar.
13. The **CDA**: what it is (a notional account) and how John's fills over time.
14. Real case: **the Rogers family** ($102M through the CDA).
15. Diversify: John chooses seg funds over a second rental property. **Real estate vs the S&P 500** (real figures) and the seg fund features.

## Chapter 4: Christmas, age 46–50. The engine starts (5 min)
16. John borrows against the policy to open **location #2**. The corporate dollar works twice.
17. Real case: **the famous names** (Disney, Kroc and others) used the same playbook.
18. **Debt-free at 50:** the loan and mortgage paid off faster. Net worth climbs.

## Chapter 5: The plot twists (5 min)
19. **Twist 1: a market crash** (age 48). John's seg funds have a guarantee floor, then a reset locks in gains.
20. **Twist 2: John's partner dentist at location #2 suddenly dies** (age 55). The funded buy-sell and key person cover keep the clinic running and pay the partner's family fairly.
21. The 7 Needs tracker: most ornaments now lit.

## Chapter 6: Christmas, age 55–65. Growth and freedom (4 min)
22. Three locations. His lawyer sets up the will, a holdco and an estate freeze. His accountant times the capital dividends.
23. **Retirement at 65:** tax-efficient income. Lily takes over the clinic; Max is treated fairly (estate equalization).

## Chapter 7: Christmas, age 90. The legacy (3 min)
24. John passes away at 90. The policy pays the corporation, the CDA pays his family **tax-free**, and the seg funds skip probate.
25. The family Christmas: grandkids, and the legacy that stayed in the family. All 7 ornaments and all 3 rules are gold.

## Epilogue: Jake, the twin (3 min)
26. Rewind: meet Jake. Same everything. He did nothing.
27. Their two lives replayed side by side (net worth, debts, locations, the crash).
28. Real case: **the Two Doctors** ("John and Jake aren't hypothetical: here's real data").
29. At 90: what each family received. Jake's family pays the price.

## Close (2 min)
30. "John had a team. Who's on yours?"
31. Let's Build Yours (call to action).
32. Thank You + QR code. Merry Christmas.
